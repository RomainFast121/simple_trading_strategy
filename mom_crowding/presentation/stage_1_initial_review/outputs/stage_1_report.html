Stage 1 — MOM Initial Review

Execution: Modeled post-only limits stay open until the next decision. Strict trade-through in an intervening hourly bar counts as a fill. Maker fees are 2 bps on fills; funding is 1 bp/day on gross held exposure. Queue priority and partial fills are unobserved in the historical simulation.

Development calibrated the sleeve to 20% maximum drawdown before the ensemble was calibrated independently. The out-of-sample segment was not used to select its scale. The execution scenario is retrospective, not new independent confirmation.
