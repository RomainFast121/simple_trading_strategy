Stage 4 — Institutional capacity analysis

Compounded 10M reference account · gradual execution · withdrawal-only capital reviews

Purpose. This report estimates whether the confirmed ensemble can retain attractive economics at institutional size after a 5 bp direct-fill taker fee, funding, gradual execution and endogenous market impact. Maker or post-only fills are not assumed because their fill condition is difficult to justify at institutional notional. It is a retrospective capacity diagnostic, not a live execution guarantee.

Impact model and interpretation

Research-supported square-root model

σt,i = ln(Ht,i / Lt,i) / √(4 ln 2)
It,i = σt,i √(Qt,i / Vt,i)
Costimpactt,i = Qt,iIt,i

This relationship is supported by empirical market-impact research across assets and Bitcoin metaorders.

Variables and interpretation

Q is child-order notional, V is observed hourly quote volume, and H and L are the hourly high and low. The observed midpoint captures market movement while the order is worked; the square-root term estimates the expected mechanical footprint.

Method references: square-root market-impact research and Bitcoin metaorder evidence.

Economic performance and consistency

Sharpe, volatility, win rate and rolling statistics use one all-cost daily return excluding withdrawals. Annual withdrawal is the total capital removed divided by the observed period in years. Annual return, drawdown and Calmar use total economic wealth: invested account value plus retained withdrawals.

Highlighted frozen case

The highlighted configuration is a 12-hour transition with capital reviewed every four months. Each controlled sleeve and the combined book were calibrated separately on development to a drawdown ceiling of 20%. Daily refreshes only extend the OOS result.

Costs and capital

Each scheduled child order assumes direct taker execution and pays 5 bps because a maker or post-only fill condition is difficult to justify at institutional notional. The fee reduces both wealth and executed quantity. Funding charges 1 bp per day times gross exposure. Market impact is an adverse performance cost and does not alter quantity. Reviews withdraw wealth above 10M and never inject capital.
