How to read this stage

Stage 1 is the initial review layer for the frozen multi-strategy ensemble. It combines the confirmed MOM and market-neutral sleeves using a fixed private allocation. Returns are aligned at realization time and measured after each sleeve's modeled fees, with no cross-sleeve fee-netting credit. Exact private allocation parameters are intentionally not displayed.
