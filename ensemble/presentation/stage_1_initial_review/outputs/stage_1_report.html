Stage 1 — Multi-Strategy Ensemble Initial Review

Costs: Ensemble: 2 bp maker fees on modeled post-only fills and 1 bp/day funding on gross exposure. Unfilled orders retain the prior holding. Each sleeve is independently calibrated on development to a 20% maximum drawdown under the same modeled post-only execution; the combined book is then calibrated separately.

A modeled order remains open until the next decision. A strict trade through its limit in any intervening hourly bar counts as a fill; queue position and partial fills are unobserved. Caps apply to intended targets, not guaranteed holdings. This retrospective OOS scenario is not fresh confirmation.

Generated locally from ignored private allocation and strategy configurations. Exact sleeve weights and underlying operational parameters are intentionally not exported.

How to read this stage

Stage 1 is the initial review layer for the frozen multi-strategy ensemble. It combines the confirmed MOM and market-neutral sleeves using a fixed private allocation. The comparison begins when both sleeves have data, although MOM's standalone development history starts earlier. Returns are aligned at realization time. Ensemble trades are formed from the final combined book and charged once. Exact private allocation parameters are not displayed.
