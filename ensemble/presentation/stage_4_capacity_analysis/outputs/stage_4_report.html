Stage 4 — Institutional capacity analysis

Compounded 10M reference account · 12h, 18h and 24h transition schedules · withdrawal-only capital reviews

Purpose. This report estimates whether the confirmed ensemble can retain attractive economics at institutional size after transaction fees, funding, gradual execution and endogenous market impact. It is a retrospective capacity diagnostic, not a live execution guarantee.

Target and execution

The required quantity change is split equally across the first 12, 18 or 24 hourly bars.

Three distinct costs

A 5 bp transaction fee reduces both wealth and executed quantity. Funding charges 1 bp per day times gross exposure. Market impact uses coefficient one as an adverse performance cost and does not alter the quantity held.

Capital reviews

Every one or four months, wealth above 10M is withdrawn and retained outside the trading account. If the account is below 10M, nothing happens: capital is never injected.

Risk calibration

Each sleeve is independently scaled on development economic wealth to at most 20% drawdown, then the combined ensemble receives its own development-only scale. Those values are frozen OOS onward.

Impact model and interpretation

Research-supported square-root model

σt,i = ln(Ht,i / Lt,i) / √(4 ln 2)
It,i = Y σt,i √(Qt,i / Vt,i),  Y = 1
Costimpactt,i = Qt,i It,i

This square-root relationship is supported by empirical market-impact research across assets and by Bitcoin metaorder evidence.

Variables

Q is the child-order notional executed during the hour and V is that hour's observed perpetual-futures quote volume. H and L are the hourly high and low; σ is the matching Parkinson range-volatility estimate. I is the estimated fractional adverse price impact.

Interpretation

The observed execution midpoint captures favourable or adverse market movement while the order is worked. The additional square-root term estimates the expected mechanical footprint and is charged as a non-negative implementation cost. It is an average-impact model rather than a tick-level order-book simulation.

Method references: square-root market-impact research and Bitcoin metaorder evidence.

Economic performance and consistency

Sharpe, volatility, win rate and rolling statistics use one all-cost daily return excluding withdrawals. Annual withdrawal is the total capital removed divided by the observed period in years. Annual return, drawdown and Calmar use total economic wealth: invested account value plus retained withdrawals.
